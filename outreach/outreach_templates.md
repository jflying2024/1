  line card + price list be useful? Happy to quote a trial order."
- **FU-2 (day 10, email):** "Last note from me — if MV/LV switchgear sourcing isn't a
  priority now, no problem. I'll leave our catalogue here for whenever it is: [[link]]."

## Personalization checklist (before every send)
- [ ] Confirmed the company actually fits the segment (right products, active site).
- [ ] First line references something specific to them (market/project/product).
- [ ] Product list trimmed to what *they* would buy.
- [ ] Opt-out line present.
- [ ] WhatsApp number used only because the company published it.
