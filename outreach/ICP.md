# Zhebao Electrical — Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) & Outreach Strategy

> Purpose: define *who* to target, *why* they fit, and *where* to find them through
> public, compliant channels, so the cold-outreach (开发信 / WhatsApp) lands with
> relevance instead of being treated as spam.

## 2. Ideal Customer Profiles (priority order)

### ICP-A — MV/LV switchgear distributors & importers (PRIMARY)
- **Who:** Companies that import and resell electrical distribution equipment.
- **Why fit:** Buy repeatedly, in volume, across the whole catalogue; value OEM
  pricing, IEC compliance, and a licensed Siemens/ABB/Schneider partner.
- **Pain points to hit:** stable supply, competitive FOB pricing, IEC test reports,
  fast lead time, after-sales spare parts.

### ICP-B — EPC / electrical contractors on infrastructure & energy projects
- **Who:** Turnkey contractors for substations, industrial plants, solar, mining,
  oil & gas, water/utilities.
- **Why fit:** Need prefabricated substations, MV switchgear, transformers per
  project BOQ; care about IEC compliance and on-time delivery.
- **Pain points:** project deadlines, spec compliance, emergency replacement,
  harsh-site ratings (dust, heat, altitude).

### ICP-C — Panel builders / OEM assemblers
- **Who:** Local assemblers building LV/MV boards.
- **Why fit:** Buy switchgear frames, breakers (ACB/MCCB/MCB/RCCB), components.
- **Pain points:** component availability, consistent quality, type-test backing.

### ICP-D — Large industrial end-users (mining, cement, oil & gas, manufacturing)
- **Who:** Plants that procure power equipment directly.
- **Why fit:** Need transformers, MV switchgear, power-quality/reactive-power gear
  for unstable or heavy-load grids.
- **Pain points:** uptime, power quality, equipment ruggedness, emergency spares.

## 3. Priority markets (emerging markets = Zhebao's sweet spot)

Tier 1 (start here): **UAE, Saudi Arabia, Egypt, Nigeria, Vietnam, Indonesia.**
Tier 2: Kenya, Ethiopia, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Philippines, Iraq, Algeria, Peru, Chile.

Why: high infrastructure/energy build-out, frequent grid-quality issues, price
sensitivity, and openness to Chinese IEC-compliant suppliers with brand licensing.

## 4. Where to find them (public, compliant sources only)

- Company **official websites** (they publish their own sales email / WhatsApp).
- **B2B directories:** Yellow Pages of each country, Kompass, EuroPages, TradeIndia,
  Made-in-China buyer side, industry association member lists.
- **Trade-show exhibitor/visitor directories:** Middle East Energy (Dubai),
  Electrix, Elenex, POWER-GEN, local electrical expos.
- **Tender / project portals** for EPC leads.
- **LinkedIn company pages** (to confirm company type & decision-maker titles).

Rule: only collect contact details a business **publishes itself for inbound sales
inquiries**. No scraping of private numbers, no purchased lists, no bulk auto-send.

## 5. Compliance guardrails (protect the brand & the WhatsApp number)

- Use a **WhatsApp Business** account; for any volume use the **official WhatsApp
  Business API** via a BSP (e.g. 360dialog, Twilio). Do not mass-send from a
  personal app — it gets banned.
- First message must: identify Zhebao, be **relevant** to that company, be short,
  and offer an **easy opt-out** ("reply STOP and I won't message again").
- Respect each destination country's anti-spam / privacy law (GDPR-style consent in
  many markets). Prefer **email first**, WhatsApp only where the company lists it.
- Throttle: personalize and send in small batches; never blast identical text.

## 6. Suggested workflow

1. Pick a Tier-1 market + one ICP segment.
2. Pull 20–50 companies from public sources → `prospects.csv`.
3. Verify each is a real fit (right product segment, active site).
4. Personalize the matching template (segment + product focus).
5. Send **email first**; follow up on WhatsApp only if the company publishes it.
6. Track replies; double down on the segments/markets that respond.
