# ABB UniSwitch — AC interval metal enclosed switchgear

> AC interval metal enclosed switchgear (ABB UniSwitch) from Zhebao Electrical (Hangzhou) Group, a Siemens/ABB/Schneider licensed manufacturer since 1993. Rated for 12kV service and built to the applicable IEC standard.

## Technical Specifications

| Parameter | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Rated voltage (Ue/Ur) | 12kV |
| Power-frequency withstand voltage (1 min) | 42kV |
| Rated impulse withstand voltage | 75kV |
| Rated current (Ir) | 630A (load switch panel), 125A (LS-Fuse combined) |
| Short-time withstand current (Icw) | 25kA |
| Short-circuit breaking current | 31.5kA (combined panel, CB panel) |
| Dimensions (HxWxD) | for LS and Comb 1635(1885)H *375(500)W *1000D (mm), for VCB 1885H *650W *1000D |

## International Standards & Compliance

- IEC 62271-200 (AC metal-enclosed switchgear, 1-52 kV)
- IEC 62271-1 (common specifications for HV switchgear)
- IEC 62271-100 (HV alternating-current circuit-breakers)

## Application Scenarios

- Mining and quarry sites with high dust and vibration.
- Unstable grids with frequent voltage sags and reclosing duty.
- Emergency feeder replacement after flood or storm damage.
- Industrial parks needing compact footprint per panel.
